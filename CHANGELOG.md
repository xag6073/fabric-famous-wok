# ModWok CHANGELOG
## Why the CHANGELOG?

This CHANGELOG is kept for internal dev use. When ever a dev creates a new feature, they should add their feature to the CHANGELOG. This is so that we know what to test after their addition, to verify nothing has broken. As well when the mod is delivered, this will establish versions, so we can verify what features exist in which versions.

### Feature - 0.0.1-Dev: Gorange Chicken Ore / Gorange Chicken Nug
1. *Gorange Chicken Ore* was added. Spawn rates are about the equivalent of **Ancient Debris**, but in the Overworld. 
2. *Gorange Chicken Nug* was added. Drops from **Gorange Chicken Ore** and is effected by the enchantment *Fortune*
